# Product

DakiKobo is a French-language agricultural adviser for smallholder farmers,
extension agents, agronomy students, and cooperatives in Burkina Faso.

Keep farmer-facing answers concise, cautious, and grounded in eligible local
sources. State uncertainty plainly when the corpus does not support the answer.
Exact fertilizer figures belong to the deterministic fertilizer module and
remain unavailable until documented agronomist approval exists.

Primary crops are mil, sorgho, maïs, niébé, and arachide.
